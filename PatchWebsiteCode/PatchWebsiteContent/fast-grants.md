# Patch Grants

**Quick Patch Grants of €200–€1,000 for young builders in Ireland — go build.**

Patch Grants support young builders in Ireland (ages **13–19**) with fast, flexible funding to help you take action and learn by doing.

Whether you want to:

- Build a prototype or hardware project  
- Run a small community event or workshop  
- Travel to a conference or learning opportunity  
- Explore an idea you can’t shake  

…we’ll fund anything that meaningfully helps you learn by building real things.

---

## How it works

- **Grant size:** €200–€1,000  
- **Turnaround:** ~1 week  
- **Process:** Short form → 15–30 min call → decision  

These Patch Grants are designed to help early builders get started fast — before you have a network, track record, or budget. Even small projects can unlock confidence, skills, and a lifelong builder identity.

---

## Examples of what we’ve funded

- A **direct air capture prototype** that went on to win *Top Individual* at BTYSE  
- **Give(a)Go** — a monthly hands-on tech meetup now supported by industry partners  
- **Travel to a 1517 Fund conference** in Los Angeles, leading to a startup internship in San Francisco  
- **Custom PCB + electronics** for NFC-powered cufflinks  

At Patch, we believe young people can build real things, right now — and we want to help you start.

## FAQs

### Who can apply for a grant?
Young people aged **13–19** in Ireland who want to build, create, or make something happen.

---

### What can I apply with?
Anything that helps you learn, build, and push yourself.

We fund:
- Hardware & engineering projects  
- Community events & workshops  
- Travel for learning and exposure  
- Creative technical projects  
- Early-stage experiments and prototypes  

You do **not** need a polished idea. Curiosity + action count.

---

### How much funding can I get?
**€200–€1,000.**

If your project needs more, we’ll help you think through other funding options.

---

### What’s the process?
Fast and lightweight:
1. Short online form  
2. 15–30 minute call  
3. Decision within ~1 week  

---

### Do I get mentorship?
Yes. We connect you with someone helpful in your domain when relevant. These Patch Grants are about momentum, not bureaucracy — mentorship is tailored and lightweight.

---

### What do you look for?
- Curiosity and initiative  
- A desire to learn by doing  
- Follow-through  
- Ambition to stretch yourself  

---

### Can I reapply?
Yes! If your idea evolves or you have a new one, you’re welcome to apply again.
